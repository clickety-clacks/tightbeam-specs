### INCIDENT part 2 — the circuit does not gate credential installation

The incident had two mechanisms, not one. Part 1 stopped the rollback discarding
an installed credential. Part 2 is the other half: the adapter circuit breaker
refused `start_provider_runtime`, so even a credential that survived could not be
activated without an operator restarting the gateway.

**Mike's decomposition (the fix):** the circuit protects AGENT CONNECTIONS from a
dead harness; it has no authority over CREDENTIAL INSTALLATION. Onboarding writes
adapter health, it does not ask permission. Conflating them deadlocked recovery
by construction — the circuit is guaranteed open exactly when a credential has
stopped working, which is the only reason anyone replaces one.

**The change is one clause:** `entry.circuit == :open and not authoritative? ->`.

**Six discarded versions, and why that is the lesson.** The first attempt DELETED
the circuit outright; Sol round 1 found the latch was silently pacing
demand-driven starts that backoff does not pace, so deleting it traded a cage for
a spin — reverted. The next five rounds each applied a finding by ADDING
machinery: fence reordering, a bespoke close skipping the park ceremony, a
generation reset, a named lifecycle API. Round 6 found the decisive fact:
**`fenced?/2` counts durable `running` rows, not only park fences, so the fence
reordering would have refused EVERY activation with a live adapter in
production** — and the new tests could not see it because they omitted
`process_identity_dir` and so never created the row every real adapter has.

Mike's ruling: **"the minimum always."** Six rounds of real findings is a signal
about SHAPE, not evidence of thoroughness — nearly every finding concerned
machinery the fix had added rather than the original bug. Cutting back to the
single clause retired all four outstanding findings at once, because they were
all about the additions. Every pre-existing close, fence, park and
death-accounting behaviour is untouched.

**Review:** Sol (gpt-5.6-sol) high, seven rounds. Final verdict on the minimal
patch: *"Safe to merge... I found no blocking or important defect introduced by
the clause."* Its one non-blocking note — that the test comments overstated what
a coordinator-level admission test proves — was applied.
