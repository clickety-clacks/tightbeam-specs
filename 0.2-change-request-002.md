## The picture, in plain words

Expensive minds should think, and cheap desks should coordinate. Every
costly agent (a worker: coder, reviewer, product owner, orchestrator) can
have a front desk (an exec): a fast, cheap agent that handles its
messages, guards its attention, and does its mechanical chores. Desks are
wired to each other, parent to child, and that wiring IS the org's nervous
system. Nobody builds the network; it grows as a side effect of hiring,
because hiring always creates a mind and its desk together, already
connected to the desk above. Minds judge. Desks execute. Machinery
(the prodder floor, dispatch rules) guarantees the floor under everyone.

## Decisions, with what each one adds or changes

**D1 — The office is the unit of hiring.**
A hire creates mind + desk together, and wires the new desk to the
spawner's desk.
ADD: office templates in the neutral seed (role charter + desk
delegation-card template, paired); spawn-and-wire mechanics in the desk
playbook. Fabric §4 (office pattern) and §6 amend at r5.

**D2 — Minds decide, desks execute; spawns cite authority.**
A desk may spawn only as directed execution of its principal's recorded
decision. The spawn filing must cite the authority row (the attest or
ruling it executes); uncited spawns are refused by rule.
ADD: a dispatch rule template (seed): desk-archetype sessions' spawn verbs
require an authority reference. Fabric §6 verb law amends at r5.

**D3 — Exec containment is environmental, three layers.**
Desks are cheap minds we do not trust with substance, so limits are walls,
not wisdom: (1) provisioning — desk-class homes ship with no repository
and only throwaway scratch; (2) law — per-archetype dispatch rules: no
substance verdicts, no holding or receiving implementation cards, no
completions off the delegation card; (3) audit — desk overreach is a
specimen class Miller probes.
CHANGE: the archetype manifest gains a provisioning election (desk vs
workshop). ADD: archetype-keyed dispatch rules (mechanism is contentless;
the restrictive matrix ships prearmed on the seed exec archetype and any
org may loosen it by ordinary law). Fabric §6/§8 amend at r5.

**D4 — A desk's memory is rows.**
Working state lives as attests on its own delegation card (latest-wins per
key); durable documents become artifacts via artifact-record; scratch is
disposable. Restaffing a desk costs one spawn and zero knowledge: the
successor reads the card and resumes.
ADD: notes-in-rows discipline in the desk playbook. Fabric §6 amends.

**D5 — One desk archetype; pairing is priced, not prescribed.**
Desk craft is domain-free, so ONE seed archetype serves all principals.
Frontier-class minds get a dedicated desk; mid-tier squads share a pool
desk; the cheapest workers get bones only. Desks have no desks: the
prodder floor below and the principal above are their only guardians.
Fabric §6 granularity text confirms at r5.

**D6 — Desks bend by identity composition, four knobs. (RATIFIED as the
build target.)**
(1) The neutral seed ships the desk archetype's core guidance — the
invariant craft. (2) Kungfu bundles contribute desk guidance fragments,
installed by `learn`, merged by `identity relearn`, pushed live by
`identity apply`. (3) Worker archetype manifests gain a `desk` section: a
guidance fragment the principal's archetype contributes to its OWN desk's
identity, composed into the desk's projected home at office creation — the
coder's job description teaches the coder's receptionist. (4) Standing
directives — attests on the delegation card, latest-wins — are the sticky
notes. Beneath all four, dispatch rules and gate statutes stay hard.
CHANGE: archetype manifest schema (`desk` section) and home projection
(compose the principal's desk fragment at office creation). This is the
one real substrate mechanism change in this CR. New fabric section at r5;
implementation lands with Phase 2.

**D7 — The inception SOP (how a product is born).**
A product-scale request (from the user OR an agent) opens a huddle work
item and spawns a Product Owner. The PO runs a structured spirit interview
— the requester is summoned via an input-needed decision-request (the
gh#11 create-path, with its conversation anchor), in plain STE-style
language. The output is a spirit charter: a document in the spec commons
holding the product's invariants, non-goals, and taste rulings, recorded
via artifact-record and bound to the product's work items via spec-ref.
The PO rules "spirit ratified"; the PO's DESK then spawns the orchestrator
and its desk, wires them in, and hands over the charter binding.
ADD: inception SOP in seed guidance; spirit-interview protocol in the PO
playbook; charter template in the commons conventions. Fabric gains an
inception section at r5.

**D8 — The altitude rule (who runs the work).**
The PO judges; the orchestrator coordinates. The orchestrator archetype's
charter gains the lifecycle contract: it owns the LIFETIME of its work
items and their toplines — staffing, sequencing, review commissioning,
dispositions — under the PO's spirit rulings. POs do not assign
implementation cards directly. Ships as a seed statute template an org may
arm. Evidence of need: 0.1 POs staffing coders and running reviews
themselves while orchestrators sat idle.
ADD: orchestrator charter amendment (seed); altitude statute template.

**D9 — Cross-reference.** CR-001's items (prodder/Avasarala naming, nurse
contract, Miller-as-playbook, promotion tiers, first-boot staffing,
no-acknowledgment law) execute in the same r5 sitting. Nothing in CR-002
alters the in-flight Phase 1 build.

## The workflows these produce

- **W1 Inception:** request → huddle + PO spawn → spirit interview (user
  summoned by decision-request) → charter written, recorded, bound → PO
  ratifies → PO's desk spawns orchestrator + desk, wires the graph.
- **W2 Hiring:** orchestrator decides a role is needed → its desk spawns
  the office from seed templates, citing the decision row → new desk wired
  to parent desk at birth.
- **W3 A day at the desk:** triage inbound by class → batch the routine →
  summon the principal only for judgment → file everything on the card.
- **W4 Bending a desk:** ship it (seed) / learn it (kungfu) / inherit it
  (worker archetype's desk section) / note it (standing directive).
- **W5 Violation path:** a desk oversteps → the filing is refused with the
  rule named → the refusal is specimen material → Miller's probes count it.
- **W6 Restaffing:** desk dies or is replaced → successor spawned from the
  same template reads the delegation card → resumes mid-shift; nothing
  was lost because nothing lived only in the desk.
