# 0.2 Change Request 003 — Landing: the mergedown card and the world-fact law

Status: AUTHORIZED (Mike, 2026-08-14). Executor: the 0.2 orchestrator, as
folding authority. Enters the Phase 3 queue; it must land before the closure
path hardens, because it changes what "delivered" means. Source: the
2026-08-13/14 investigation of the live org's merge floor, summarized in §0.

## §0 — What went wrong, in rows

The live org held 66 work items carrying completion attests AND reviewed-clean
verdicts. Reconstructing what had actually shipped took an evening and produced
four wrong answers along the way:

- 24 were already on the branch, awaiting only closure.
- 4 were genuinely unmerged.
- 13 recorded commits that resolve on exactly one machine (spawner scratch
  paths), so nothing could be concluded about them at all.
- 24 were rulings, recon or guidance with nothing to merge.
- 2 had landed as cherry-picks under rewritten SHAs, so ancestry testing
  called them missing until patch identity was checked.

No attest kind means "merged". `verified` sits on all four genuinely unmerged
items; it means a gate passed, not that code reached the branch. The verdict
vocabulary is open text with 200+ minted values, none of them `merged` or
`landed`. The merge truth, where it exists, lives in prose inside integrator
lanes' notes, which no query reaches.

The cost is not only external. One item spent ~36 hours filing read-only
rechecks trying to establish whether a repair was live in the installed
runtime. Producers retire on completion, so by the time an item is ready its
branch context is usually gone.

## §1 — The picture, in plain words

Work is not done when someone says it is done. It is done when it is in the
world, and somebody wrote that down at the moment it became true.

Two obligations chain. When final review clears, the owner must open a
**mergedown card**. The prodder will not go quiet until that card exists. The
mergedown card will not close until a **landing receipt** exists on it. Between
them, work cannot silently stall in the gap between "approved" and "shipped",
which is where it has been stalling.

The substrate never merges, never opens the card, and never picks a holder. It
only refuses to be quiet.

## §2 — Decisions

**D1 — Landing is a recorded fact, not a reconstruction.**
A landing receipt records: the typed external reference (canonical remote plus
commit, never a host-local worktree path), the probe that proved it, a digest
of what the probe returned, who ran it, when, and how long it stays good.
ADD: `merged` as a reserved verdict kind; landing receipts as a source kind on
the existing receipt bone (see D7). Promote, do not compete with, the org's own
`merge-readiness-approval-handoff-<hash>.md`, which already requires that
tests-passed, reviewed-clean and completion agree on one exact repository and
commit. This CR supplies the half it stops short of.

**D2 — Unknown is a legal answer, and absence of evidence is never evidence
of absence.**
A probe that cannot reach its subject files `unknown`, never `not landed`. This
is the rails mechanism's existing invariant (never fabricate a verdict, never
guess a value for a check that could not complete, `unreported` proves no
verdict exists) extended from statute checks to the facts work items depend on.
Both failures observed in §0 were false certainty: `verified` reading as
shipped, and an unresolvable ref reading as absent.

**D3 — The mergedown card is opened by a mind, and prodded into existence.**
When final review clears, a statute observes the readiness condition and prods
the accountable owner to open a mergedown card. The prod stops on one thing
only: the card exists. The substrate does not create it and does not choose its
holder, so agent-first holds; but forgetting is no longer silent.
ADD: readiness-to-card statute; prod subject = the owner's own open lifecycle
card on that work item.

**D4 — Accountable owner: the orchestrator that owns the item's lifecycle.**
The PO opens it where no orchestrator sits between it and the work, and remains
accountable for its orchestrators either way (CR-002 D8 already places work item
and topline lifetimes in the orchestrator charter).

**D5 — The holder must be alive.**
Producers' cards close on completion and those sessions retire, so the producer
is usually gone by readiness. Open the mergedown card on the producer if it
still lives, otherwise hire a custodian office for it (CR-002 D1). Opening a
card on a retired session recreates the orphan class this CR exists to close.

**D6 — The seam: a no-landing declaration discharges the obligation.**
Rulings, recon, guidance and huddles have nothing to merge; 24 of the 66 were
exactly this. The owner may discharge by filing a no-landing declaration with
its evidence instead of opening a card. Work already on the branch under a
rewritten SHA discharges the same way, citing patch identity: during the
2026-08-13 sweep a custodian correctly refused a merge on those grounds, and a
rail without this seam would have prodded that agent into a redundant merge
forever.

**D7 — Generalize the receipt bone; it already exists, pointed inward.**
`supervision_liveness_receipts` (560 live rows) already carries receiptId,
typed source kind and id, source time, acceptance time, generation and
`expiresAt`; `recurrence_suppression_receipts` already carries a fingerprint
digest. The staleness idea is already proven in production. What is missing is
only the direction they point: every existing receipt cites an internal durable
row, and a landing receipt must cite an external observation. ADD: external
observation as a source kind, carrying probe + output digest. This is a graft,
not an invention.

**D8 — The world-fact law lands in kungfu, with landing as its first
instance.**
> When you observe a fact about the world that others will act on, record it at
> the moment you observe it, with the command that proves it, a digest of what
> came back, and how long it stays good. If you cannot reach the thing, file
> unknown. Never file the absence of evidence as evidence of absence.

Siblings already costing the org, each solved ad hoc and separately: is the fix
live in the installed runtime (liveness receipts); is the gateway up (operators
told to check, never assume); is there provider capacity (rediscovered by
failing a turn, per-agent); does this artifact resolve off-host (an artifact
recorded a path with no host and had to be hand-copied and re-hashed); is my
base current (base-sync gate); did CI pass; is this credential real (doctor
checks); is that external ticket open. Five bespoke mechanisms exist for one
pattern that has never been named. Naming it is the point: the next instance
should cost nothing.
ADD to `priv/kungfu/agentic-engineering/guidance/`: the law in the engineering
tenets, and the landing instance in the coder, reviewer and orchestrator
guidance. Note the nearest existing tenet, "verify from rows, never from a
worker's self-report", is the right instinct aimed at the wrong target: rows
carry self-reports. Amend it.

**D9 — Escalation ceiling.**
When prods exhaust at either stage, escalate to the stall patrol for
adjudication against its casebook, never to Mike, per the superseding
stall-escalation law.

## §3 — Hazard, and a dependency

Do not build this on the current prodder. `wi_e27eb1a6` ("prodder exhausts
retry tiers after failed turns") is open, which means the floor can go silent
exactly when a lane is struggling. A landing rail standing on a silent floor
strands merges while looking supervised, which is worse than today. The prodder
unbraid is Phase 4 work; this CR's statutes must either follow it or carry
their own liveness proof. Note the irony for the ledger: that item is itself one
of the four awaiting a merge.

## §5 — Relationship to the live 0.1 line

The kungfu half needs no substrate change and applies to the live org today:
the owner opens a mergedown card when final review clears, held by a live
custodian, closing only on a `merged` verdict carrying the remote readback, or
on a no-landing declaration. Relayed to both product owners as a Mike-directed
ruling on 2026-08-14. 0.1 gains the practice now; 0.2 gains the floor that makes
forgetting impossible.

## §6 — Scope: general floor, one probe, empty slot

This is deliberately not a merge feature. Build in this order and no wider:

1. **The floor is general and contentless.** The external-observation receipt
   knows nothing of git, branches, or merging: it carries a typed reference, the
   probe, an output digest, an expiry, and a representable `unknown`. If the
   word "merge" appears anywhere in the substrate half of this change, the split
   is wrong.
2. **Exactly one probe ships, and it ships as kungfu.** The git landing probe
   lives in `agentic-engineering`, where domain content belongs. A shop on
   Perforce, or one where landing means "the release is serving traffic", swaps
   the probe and touches nothing else.
3. **The SEED's probe list ships EMPTY; ENGINEERING KUNGFU's does not.**
   This line matters and is easy to get backwards. The seed is what every new
   org of any domain inherits, so it ships the shape with no answers (razor,
   fabric §5b/§8b). Engineering kungfu is a domain bundle for software shops,
   and known probes are exactly the learned content it exists to carry. Ship
   them there.

4. **Patch the known gaps now, in both lines.** We have row-level evidence for
   each of these already; leaving them unwritten repeats the mistake this CR
   documents. Add to `agentic-engineering` as guidance with probe shapes, not
   as machinery:

   | world-fact | probe shape | stays good until |
   |---|---|---|
   | code landed | ancestry + patch identity against the canonical remote, `ls-remote` readback | the branch tip moves |
   | the fix is live in the installed runtime | version/build stamp read back from the RUNNING service, not the repo | restart or upgrade |
   | the service is up | `is-active` / `GET /version` against the live endpoint | minutes |
   | provider capacity | the refusing turn's own limit message: record pool + reset time | the stated reset |
   | an artifact resolves off-host | sha256 readback from a second host | the artifact changes |
   | the base is current | recorded base sha vs remote tip | the tip moves |
   | CI verdict | status for the EXACT pushed sha | the sha changes |
   | a credential works | doctor/validate call against the provider | short |

   Do not go hunting for a ninth. That is Miller's promotion scouting, and the
   general floor makes the next instance cost a probe rather than a subsystem.

**Sequencing is the load-bearing part.** 0.2 currently inherits 0.1's closure
semantics unchanged, and therefore inherits this hole. Once Phase 3 hardens what
"delivered" means, this stops being a fold and becomes a migration. Land the
floor before the closure path sets.

## §7 — Make `tightbeam-law-minting` the design-pattern catalog

Today the skill is a working projection of wisdom rules 1-10 and 25: correct,
but doctrine-shaped. A rail author needs named patterns with the failure each
one prevents, so that minting starts from "which pattern is this?" instead of
from a blank page. Doctrine stays canonical in `wisdom.md` and is still cited by
number; the skill becomes the applied vocabulary. Every pattern below was paid
for in rows.

**Patterns**

1. **Obligation, not notification.** If you want work to happen, it must exist
   as a card. Prods key on assignments, so a notice is unprodded and evaporates.
   *Prevents:* the readiness notice nobody owes — 66 items sat approved and
   unshipped behind exactly this.
2. **Prod to existence.** When a rail wants an obligation created, it prods the
   accountable mind until the row exists rather than creating it. Stop condition
   is the row, not a reply. *Prevents:* the substrate manufacturing obligations
   and choosing holders, which is control.
3. **Typed exit condition.** A card closes on a named fact, never on an
   assertion that the work is done. *Prevents:* completion as self-report.
4. **The inhibition seam.** Every mandatory rail ships a lawful discharge for
   the cases it does not apply to, recorded with its evidence. *Prevents:* the
   forever-prod — 24 of those 66 items had nothing to merge, and one custodian
   correctly refused a redundant merge that a seamless rail would have forced.
5. **Unknown is a value.** A check that cannot reach its subject files unknown.
   Absence of evidence is never evidence of absence. *Prevents:* false
   certainty in both directions (`verified` reading as shipped; an unresolvable
   ref reading as absent). Already implemented as the `unreported` sentinel —
   reuse it, do not reinvent it.
6. **Observation over testimony.** A fact about the world carries the probe that
   proves it, a digest of what came back, and how long it stays good.
   *Prevents:* reconstruction archaeology, and testimony that cannot be
   challenged.
7. **Escalation ceiling.** Exhaustion routes to the nurse/patrol for
   adjudication against its casebook, never to the human except by the human's
   own standing law. *Prevents:* the org paging its operator as a control loop.
8. **Floor, not ceiling.** A rail is a pathology detector, never a limit on real
   work. One false positive is a defective rail. *Prevents:* mechanisms that
   tax the honest path.
9. **No blocking judgment.** Scripts never await inference or a session.
   *Prevents:* the deterministic layer deadlocking on a mind.
10. **Seed values as data, not constants.** Floors, ceilings and timings land as
    policy data an org can bend. *Prevents:* shipping one org's tuning as
    everyone's physics (razor).
11. **Replay identity.** The same receipt replayed returns its original outcome
    across generations. *Prevents:* the recurrence class — earned in full by
    `wi_3769fe6c` over five review rounds.
12. **One durable explanation per attempt.** Every mechanical attempt is
    explainable from rows without re-deriving it. *Prevents:* unattributable
    machinery.
13. **Atomic consequence.** The fact and what follows from it commit in one
    transaction. *Prevents:* a readiness that exists while its notice does not,
    or the reverse.
14. **Restart pickup.** Rails are software; their state survives reboot and
    resumes. *Prevents:* obligations that quietly die with a process.

**Anti-patterns, named so they can be refused in review**

- **The bespoke mechanism.** Before minting, ask whether this is an instance of
  an existing floor. Five separate mechanisms already exist here for one pattern
  nobody had named (see §0, D8).
- **Testimony laundering.** "Verify from rows, never from a worker's
  self-report" is right in spirit but rows carry self-reports; verifying from a
  row that contains a claim is still trusting the claim.
- **The threshold-guessing detector.** No evidence-free violation detector
  (wisdom 3).
- **The forever-prod.** A mandatory rail with no seam (pattern 4).
