**Approved within the existing invariant:** an explicit durable binding from a work item to its addressed-PO scope, and exactly one current accountable delivery owner for that scope. Use the existing canonical pair `(ownerUserId, poRole)` as the explicit office identity; do not introduce a generic product registry. Existing per-item owner draft alone is insufficient. No narrower supported current mechanism was demonstrated: inspected e288 work_items has human ownership only; session_po_associations is per session, permits several sessions addressing the same role, and permits an active session to set its own association. That last permission makes association alone unsuitable as commissioning authority.

This ruling approves bounded semantics, not the draft or a particular schema. The existing enforcement owner must bind its correction to these conditions and explicitly disposition the current stop-before-commit/test before implementation proceeds beyond it. PO has not run tests, approved a candidate, staffed, or edited source. Keep the prompt-only consultation notice correction separately owned and preserve its association/replay/atomic/nonblocking mechanics.

## Scope identity and minimal facts

1. The binding is an explicit authorized assertion that this item belongs to this addressed-PO delivery scope, not an inference from a title, role suffix, archetype, shared user, spawnedBy or current_parent. The pair identifies a canonical registered same-owner role office, not the current PO occupant. Rebinding the PO office to a successor session must not create a second delivery scope or lose items.
2. Two work items explicitly bound to the same pair share one current accountable delivery owner. A different pair remains a different scope even for the same human owner. This represents product accountability through the explicitly chosen PO office; it does not discover product identity from strings. Shared same-role office means shared delivery scope. Do not silently partition it by session or item to allow two PDOs. If a real use case needs distinct products sharing one undifferentiated office, return that exact ambiguity before adding another identifier or weakening uniqueness; no such requirement is established here.
3. Retain one authoritative current scope-owner fact and an explicit item-to-scope binding, with attributable cause/principal, revision/concurrency evidence and history. A per-item owner cache may not become a second independent authority. Storage layout and reuse of existing events are implementation choices; avoid a second shadow ledger.
4. Bind any association provenance to BOTH the exact association session and its revision. Revision 3 on session A is not revision 3 on session B and is not a global product generation. Scope identity stays the canonical pair. Validate current association/role/owner at admission; a stale association reference may not authorize a new claim. Replaying an old successful operation returns its recorded outcome without restoring old owner or binding state.
5. A session changing its addressed PO does not automatically move existing items, transfer the scope office or acquire commissioning authority. Existing work remains durably owned. Where a current owner's association no longer matches, require explicit reconciliation before new affected commissioning, while preserving completion/failure reporting and reachable recovery. An explicit authorized item-scope reassignment must reconcile open delegation and obligations; never cascade a session change over history.

## Authority and bootstrap

The caller needs actual authority for the named scope/work, beyond shared user ownership or possession of an association. An ordinary session cannot self-associate and then mint a scope owner, move an item, nominate itself, or manufacture a delegation to pass admission. A delegation must identify the active governing assignment/work and its existing authorized grantor, within the declared scope. A recorded topology decision guides staffing but is not itself a capability token; preserve returned PO decisions with attribution without a special author-identity gate.

Initial owner setup uses the human owner/admin or the owner's actual Main acting under existing supported bootstrap authority. This is a narrowly bounded setup/adoption path, not permission for Main/PO to commission production workers. Do not equate every same-user agent, an admin-owned agent or the orchestrator archetype with Main/bootstrap authority. Reuse existing suitable PDO and PO offices. After initialization, the current scope owner or explicitly authorized scope-custody successor can bind incoming items and delegate only within its authority; ordinary lane ownership of one item does not grant adoption of every unrelated item.

Preserve lawful creation of owner/PO intake and consultation obligations before a full production tree exists. Permit filing an unbound backlog item without starting delivery; first engineering production commissioning must resolve the explicit binding and current owner or refuse with a usable responsible-owner/bootstrap remedy. Exempt ordinary coordination, evidence and PO consultation only to the extent of their actual effect; default-archetype, omitted work item, role rename or labeling production as coordination must not confer authority. Do not claim the substrate can infer hidden production from arbitrary prose or prevent unrecorded file writes: protect observable commissioning facts and test effect misclassification only where records establish the mismatch. Do not add human confirmation for routine already-authorized binding/delegation.

Several sessions may legitimately address the same PO. Do not impose uniqueness on session_po_associations itself or prohibit lanes, specialists or a staged successor from carrying the same association. The unique fact is the current accountable owner of the explicit delivery scope.

## Succession and recovery

Use a supported explicit, attributable succession operation with an expected current owner/revision. Validate an active compatible successor and its association to the same scope. Concurrent competing claims cannot both become current; mutation and replay recording must be atomic. Preserve the predecessor record and all assignment opener/holder identities, role history, immutable spawn provenance and accepted evidence. Do not implement generic reparenting or modify wi_25e38cf9 through this relation.

A successor session may exist and prepare while the incumbent remains accountable. At the committed custody cutover, exactly one is current; merely spawning, binding a role or receiving a notice does not complete succession. If the incumbent cannot act, existing owner/Main recovery authority must provide a lawful takeover route. Do not require a dead owner's acknowledgement, automatically elect a replacement or make retirement silently delete accountability. An unavailable owner is an explicit incomplete-delivery/recovery condition, not permission to leave zero accountable scope records or silently pick a same-user session.

Open obligations must remain attributable and reachable through the established delivery/recovery path across succession. Reconcile existing assignment-bound delegation explicitly: either carry it with an attributable accepted handoff or withdraw future commissioning power while retaining completion/failure reporting and recovery. Prior delegation or an old topology verdict must not authorize a superseded owner to commission new work. Do not invalidate useful completed evidence or strand running workers merely to enforce a new-owner label. Demonstrate actual recipient/recovery behavior, not only database uniqueness or a sent notification.

- Multiple associated lane/specialist sessions and a staged successor remain legal without becoming a second current PDO. PO office occupant replacement preserves scope/item identities.

- Bootstrap owner/PO setup and consultation work; file-only backlog stays nonexecuting; first production without binding has a concrete remedy. An already-authorized intake needs no repeated human approval.
- Exercise succession with two items and existing open workers, normal completion, failure and unavailable owner; demonstrate a current accountable recovery recipient while preserving historical authorship/opener/provenance. No reparent workaround.
- Competing owner claims, stale expected revisions, association replacement, same numeric revisions on different sessions, repeated requests, conflicting request-key reuse and crash/restart between mutation and acknowledgement retain exactly one current owner and correct item bindings. Old replay cannot resurrect an incumbent.
- Preserve existing addressed-PO atomic notice/replay/restart/name-noninference guarantees; prompt changes do not add acknowledgement or delivery gates. The relation must not silently restrict unrelated SDLC-neutral work.

Use the existing two lanes and actual code/guidance review assignments. Run proportionate focused and required full gates after the enforcement owner lawfully releases the corrected design hold; exact independent review and final fresh Opus consistency acceptance remain mandatory. No new worker, product registry, timer, polling service, speculative abstraction or additional approval stage is part of this decision.
