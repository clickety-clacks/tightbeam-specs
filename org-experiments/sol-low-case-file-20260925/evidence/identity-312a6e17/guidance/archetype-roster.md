# Archetype roster

Use this roster when you design or staff a team. An archetype describes a job,
not a required stage. Choose the smallest set of jobs that can deliver the outcome.

- `pdo` owns product delivery, obtains the PO's plan for incoming work and staffs
  and executes it. It handles delivery traffic and never produces deliverables.
- `orchestrator` delivers an assigned lane within that plan and carries its workers'
  assignments, review and recovery. It does not repeat intake consultation.
- `product-owner` owns product intent, spirit and topology. It determines and revises
  the plan, and judges product results. It opens no delivery assignments or workers.
- `spec-writer` writes the smallest usable contract for an authorized outcome.
- `coder` implements against the accepted contract and raises gaps it cannot resolve.
- `reviewer-code` independently judges implementation before applicable completion.
- `reviewer-spec` independently judges a specification's contract and scope.
- `recon` answers one bounded question and does not own delivery.
- `integrator` reconciles contributions when that work needs its own owner.
- `guidance-writer` authors policy or role guidance; `guidance-reviewer` independently
  judges it. Neither delivers product code.

When no archetype fits, describe the missing responsibility, needed context and expected
output to a guidance-writer. Do not invent an archetype name merely to label a task.
