**Coverage verdict.** The corpus (operating-manual.md, seed operating-model.md, 14 archetype guidance files) covers three of the five fumble classes fully and in unusually strong, specific prose — staffing hygiene, generic-attest discipline, and parent-side block classification are all written down almost verbatim against the observed misbehavior, and the rows show the misbehavior anyway (57 never-worked, 27 zombies growing from 26, chains of verbatim-banned "NO-CHANGE HOLD" attests). That is direct evidence for the founding thesis: documentation cannot produce coordination discipline; bones do. Two genuine doc gaps exist — the holder-side surrender/blocked/handoff recipe (where the manual's own example note teaches the fumble) and role-addressed assignment discipline — plus one deferred amendment that cannot be written until the Phase 0/1 repair verbs exist. Per the §2 disposition rule these gaps neither prove nor save the thesis, which stands or falls on Miller's E-probes; the seed operating-model.md is identity-seam mechanics only and contributes nothing to any class, which is fine — it never claimed to.

**Phase 2 amendment items:**

1. **operating-manual.md §"Track work: work-items, assignments, facts"** — replace the surrender example note (`"blocked on device access"`) and add the surrender/blocked/handoff recipe: a blocker is reported and held (blocker attest + continuation wake) or escalated per the unblocking flow; surrender is a truthful terminal give-back of unfinished work; handoff is never custody transfer — the opener re-dispatches. One sentence it must teach: "blocked" is a state you report and carry; "surrendered" is a state you end in.
2. **operating-manual.md, same section** — role-addressed assignment discipline: open every assignment against a role (`--role`), never a bare session key, because a roleless assignment is invisible to `assignments --role` and to all role-history accountability. (Stopgap only; the durable fix is wi_7f068d0c's substrate snapshot repair.)
3. **operating-manual.md — deferred to Phase-1 ship** (the minimal manual amendment §2 already requires): document the agent-reachable repair verb for wedged lifecycle states (a completion whose verdict has nowhere to land, wi_1b0237fe class) the moment the Phase 0/1 verb exists — not before (wisdom 20: teach only what exists).
